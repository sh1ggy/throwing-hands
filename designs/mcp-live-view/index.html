Train operator

Train rock, paper, scissors and nothing (D9), then the 30-throw test (T7) until it passes 85 % (G2). Works whether you press the buttons in train_ui.py or ask Claude (train_gesture, read_gesture).
Operate your Mac

Claude's tool calls, the hand, LEDs and camera, the player and their mode, the round through the §9.4 states, and the raw traffic.
Stage audience

Countdown, robot vs you, score and banter, readable across the room. Never shows the player's mode, so rigged-win stays a surprise (D10).
Trace how it works

Claude → MCP server → XIAO → HuskyLens, each tool call becoming §9.2 lines and §9.6 frames. Between matches only: it shows the robot reading your throw.
